Louisville History Timeline

A deep dive into the events that shaped the Derby City.

Open the interactive version →

18th Century: The Frontier

  • 1778 · Google ↗

    George Rogers Clark establishes a settlement on Corn Island at the Falls of the Ohio during the Revolutionary War.

  • 1779 · Google ↗

    Settlers move from Corn Island to the mainland, establishing the layout of early Louisville.

  • 1780 · Google ↗

    The Virginia General Assembly formally grants the town charter, naming it Louisville in honor of King Louis XVI of France.

  • 1783 · Google ↗

    Evan Williams opens Louisville's first commercial distillery, beginning the city's long bourbon legacy.

  • 1797 · Google ↗

    Jeffersontown is founded, one of the oldest towns in Jefferson County.

19th Century: The River City Era

  • 1803 · Google ↗

    Lewis and Clark meet in Louisville/Clarksville to form the Corps of Discovery before their western expedition.

  • 1811 · Google ↗

    The town of Portland is established below the Falls of the Ohio, serving as a rival river port. The first steamboat on the Ohio River, the New Orleans, also arrives.

  • 1828 · Google ↗

    Louisville is officially incorporated as a city.

  • 1830 · Google ↗

    The Louisville and Portland Canal is completed, allowing boats to bypass the treacherous Falls of the Ohio.

  • 1837 · Google ↗

    The University of Louisville runs its first classes as the Louisville Medical Institute.

  • 1840s · Google ↗

    German and Irish immigration surges, heavily shaping the cultural and neighborhood landscapes (Butchertown, Germantown).

  • 1842 · Google ↗

    The Kentucky School for the Blind is established.

  • 1852 · Google ↗

    Louisville annexes the rival river town of Portland.

  • 1855 · Google ↗

    'Bloody Monday' anti-immigrant riots occur, leading to the deaths of at least 22 Irish and German immigrants.

  • 1858 · Google ↗

    The American Printing House for the Blind is established.

  • 1860 · Google ↗

    The Louisville Water Tower, the oldest ornamental water tower in the world, begins operation.

  • 1861 · Google ↗

    Louisville serves as a major Union supply depot and hospital base during the Civil War, deeply divided politically.

  • 1870 · Google ↗

    George Garvin Brown introduces Old Forester, the first bottled bourbon.

  • 1875 · Google ↗

    The first Kentucky Derby is run at the Louisville Jockey Club (later Churchill Downs); Aristides is the inaugural winner.

  • 1883 · Google ↗

    The Southern Exposition, a massive five-year world's fair, opens in what is now Old Louisville, largely illuminated by Thomas Edison's incandescent light bulbs.

  • 1884 · Google ↗

    John A. 'Bud' Hillerich creates the first Louisville Slugger baseball bat for player Pete Browning.

  • 1890 · Google ↗

    A devastating tornado (an estimated F4) strikes Louisville, killing at least 76 people and destroying numerous buildings downtown.

  • 1891 · Google ↗

    Mayor Charles Donald Jacob hires Frederick Law Olmsted to design a world-class park system (Cherokee, Iroquois, Shawnee).

  • 1893 · Google ↗

    Mildred and Patty Hill, two Louisville sisters, compose the melody to 'Good Morning to All', which would later become 'Happy Birthday to You'.

  • 1895 · Google ↗

    St. James Court is developed, becoming the centerpiece of Old Louisville's Victorian architecture.

20th Century: Floods, Flights, and Fights

  • 1905 · Google ↗

    The Seelbach Hotel opens, soon becoming a legendary hub for gangsters, politicians, and writers (including F. Scott Fitzgerald).

  • 1905 · Google ↗

    The Louisville Free Public Library effectively opens its doors to the public.

  • 1910 · Google ↗

    Waverly Hills Sanatorium opens to treat a severe local tuberculosis epidemic, eventually growing into a massive facility.

  • 1914 · Google ↗

    The Belle of Louisville (originally launched as the Idlewild) is built.

  • 1919 · Google ↗

    Bowman Field, now Kentucky's oldest civil airport, begins operating.

  • 1920 · Google ↗

    Prohibition begins, devastating the local distilling industry; only a few distilleries survive by selling 'medicinal' whiskey.

  • 1923 · Google ↗

    The Brown Hotel opens, soon inventing the famous 'Hot Brown' open-faced sandwich.

  • 1928 · Google ↗

    The Louisville Palace (originally the Loew's and United Artists State Theatre) opens, designed by John Eberson.

  • 1934 · Google ↗

    Kaelin's Restaurant in the Highlands allegedly invents the cheeseburger.

  • 1937 · Google ↗

    The Great Flood of 1937 devastates Louisville, submerging 70% of the city and forcing 175,000 residents from their homes.

  • 1942 · Google ↗

    Cassius Marcellus Clay Jr. (Muhammad Ali) is born in Louisville.

  • 1947 · Google ↗

    Standiford Field (now Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport) begins commercial operations.

  • 1950 · Google ↗

    Bellarmine College (now University) is founded in the Highlands.

  • 1954 · Google ↗

    General Electric opens Appliance Park, a massive manufacturing hub that reshapes the city's economy and demographics (Haier-owned since 2016).

  • 1956 · Google ↗

    The Kentucky State Fair moves to its current home at the Kentucky Exposition Center.

  • 1968 · Google ↗

    In late May, unrest erupts in the West End after a confrontation between police and Black residents, accelerating white flight and economic disinvestment.

  • 1974 · Google ↗

    An F4 tornado (part of the Super Outbreak) tears through Louisville, destroying parts of Cherokee Park and the Crescent Hill neighborhood.

  • 1975 · Google ↗

    Controversial court-ordered school busing begins in Jefferson County to achieve desegregation, sparking weeks of violent protests.

  • 1982 · Google ↗

    The Galleria opens downtown as an early revitalization effort; it is reborn as Fourth Street Live! in 2004.

  • 1982 · Google ↗

    UPS opens an air hub at Standiford Field, which will eventually become Worldport, the city's largest employer.

  • 1985 · Google ↗

    The Humana Building, designed by Michael Graves, is completed, marking a landmark in postmodern architecture.

  • 1990 · Google ↗

    Thunder Over Louisville debuts as the kickoff event to the Kentucky Derby Festival.

21st Century: Modern Louisville

  • 2000 · Google ↗

    Louisville and Jefferson County voters approve a historic city-county merger, creating Louisville Metro.

  • 2002 · Google ↗

    The Louisville Extreme Park (now David Armstrong Extreme Park) opens. The first Forecastle Festival is held modestly in Tyler Park.

  • 2003 · Google ↗

    The Louisville Metro Government officially takes effect, making Louisville the 16th largest city by population in the US.

  • 2005 · Google ↗

    The Muhammad Ali Center opens downtown.

  • 2006 · Google ↗

    21c Museum Hotel opens, pioneering a new wave of downtown boutique hospitality and contemporary art.

  • 2010 · Google ↗

    The KFC Yum! Center opens on the riverfront, becoming the new home for UofL basketball.

  • 2013 · Google ↗

    The Big Four Bridge opens to pedestrian traffic, completing a major riverfront revitalization piece stretching to Indiana.

  • 2015 · Google ↗

    The Abraham Lincoln Bridge opens downtown; the East End (Lewis and Clark) Bridge follows in late 2016.

  • 2016 · Google ↗

    Global mourning and celebration as Louisville lays its favorite son, Muhammad Ali, to rest after a massive procession through the city.

  • 2019 · Google ↗

    Standiford Field is renamed Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport.

  • 2020 · Google ↗

    Protests for racial justice center in downtown Louisville following the death of Breonna Taylor.

  • 2020 · Google ↗

    Lynn Family Stadium opens, giving Louisville City FC (and, from 2021, Racing Louisville FC) a permanent home.

  • 2022 · Google ↗

    Craig Greenberg is elected mayor.

  • 2023 · Google ↗

    The DOJ releases its findings on LMPD (March). A mass shooting at the Old National Bank downtown kills five (April 10).

  • 2024 · Google ↗

    Norton West Louisville Hospital opens, the West End's first hospital in generations. The 150th Kentucky Derby is won by Mystik Dan.

  • 2025 · Google ↗

    A historic April flood on the Ohio River forces the first-ever cancellation of Thunder Over Louisville. Sovereignty wins the 151st Derby.

  • 2025 · Google ↗

    UPS Flight 2976, an MD-11 cargo jet, crashes on takeoff on Nov. 4, killing 15. Baxter Avenue Theatres closes Dec. 31.

  • 2026 · Google ↗

    Golden Tempo wins the 152nd Derby, and Cherie DeVaux becomes the first woman to train a Derby winner (May 2).

  • 2026 · Google ↗

    The Louisville Kings win the UFL title in their first season (June 13). Mid City Mall closes for redevelopment, and WDRB and WAVE merge newsrooms.

  • 2026 · Google ↗

    The American Printing House for the Blind opens the Dot Experience museum (Oct 30).
