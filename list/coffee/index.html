Coffee

Roasters, neighborhood cafes and Yemeni coffeehouses. Where Louisville gets its caffeine.

Roasters and Cafe

  • Red Hot Roasters · Google ↗

    Irish Hill (Lexington Rd & Payne St). Organic small-batch roaster with a retro drive-thru window and retro-inspired bakery items.

  • Safai Coffee · Google ↗

    Highlands (1707 Bardstown Rd) and Logan Street Market. Direct-trade roaster since 1998, with crepes and blends named for Louisville neighborhoods.

  • Sunergos Coffee · Google ↗

    Schnitzelburg (S Preston St, next door to Nord's donuts) and Old Louisville (Woodlawn Ave). The city's first third-wave roaster, since 2004.

  • Good Folks Coffee · Google ↗

    A wholesale roaster with no cafe of its own. Look for the bags in grocery stores and in local bakeries' espresso machines.

  • Fante's Coffee · Google ↗

    Grinstead Dr, near Irish Hill. A cozy coffeehouse that roasts in house; owner Leo Fante has been in coffee since 1978. Drinks come with a tiny cookie.

  • Phalcha Coffee · Google ↗

    Germantown (Mary St). Colorful drinks and iced coffee flights.

  • Bean Roastery and Cafe · Google ↗

    Germantown (Goss Ave). Roasts its own beans; a good laptop spot, with a community printer.

  • Hinterhof · Google ↗

Cafe

  • Bayt Al Mocha · Google ↗

    Yemeni coffee from a Detroit-born chain with three Louisville shops. Get the Adeni chai or a pistachio latte.

  • Blackbeard · Google ↗

    Downtown (718 W Main, Museum Row). Started in a converted bread truck in 2014 and has roasted on Main St since 2018.

  • Blak Koffee · Google ↗

    Russell (MOLO Village, 1219 W Jefferson) and Parkland (Goodwill Opportunity Campus). Black-owned, built around an Ethiopian house blend.

  • Cup of Joy · Google ↗
  • Day's Coffee · Google ↗

    Highlands (1420 Bardstown Rd). An independent espresso bar since 1994, with couches, booths, local art and plenty of outlets.

  • Elixir Cafe · Google ↗
  • Full Stop · Google ↗

    Germantown (1132 E St. Catherine). A converted gas station with coffee, vegan-friendly food, a mini market and beer after dark.

  • Haraz · Google ↗

    Downtown (4th St, by the Brown Hotel) and Highlands (Baxter & Highland). Yemeni coffee, saffron and cardamom drinks, pistachio milk cake.

  • Haymarket River Rd · Google ↗

    River Road (3020). Haymarket by Ashbourne Farms, a cafe and market in a restored building near the Water Tower.

  • Heine Bros · Google ↗

    The local chain since 1994, all fair trade and organic. The flagship sits at Bardstown Rd & Longest Ave.

  • La Pana Bakery · Google ↗

    Smoketown (991 Logan St, next to Foko). Oaxacan-rooted bakery from fourth-generation baker Diego Hernandez, with a walk-up window.

  • Lucia's Cafecito y Mas · Google ↗

    Cherokee Triangle (1433 Bardstown Rd). Cafecito and empanadas, open since early 2026.

    Also on: Dining Directory

  • Mixmix Cafe · Google ↗

    Taylorsville Rd (2240), at the same address as My Tindahan Filipino grocery. Croffles, matcha and halo-halo.

    Also on: Dining Directory

  • Ntaba · Google ↗

    Brownsboro Rd (2407), near Clifton. A small coffee haus with a loyal following.

  • Please & Thank You · Google ↗

    NuLu (Market & Shelby) and Frankfort Ave. Coffee with the famous chocolate chip cookie.

  • Quills Coffee · Google ↗

    Local roaster. The Baxter Ave original, plus a converted firehouse on E Main St in NuLu that houses the roastery.

  • The Well · Google ↗

    Iroquois (5225 New Cut Rd), across from Iroquois Park. Comfy seats and seasonal drinks.

  • Whirling Tiger Coffee · Google ↗

    Butchertown (Story Ave), at the music venue. Moving down Story Ave in early 2027.

  • Witches Brew · Google ↗

    Clifton (1813 Frankfort Ave). Witch-themed coffee with tarot readings, open since 2024. Halloween all year.
