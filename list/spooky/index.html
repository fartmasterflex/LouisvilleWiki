🦇 Weird & Wonderful

  • The Witches' Tree (Old Louisville) · free · Google ↗

    The gnarled tree at the northwest corner of 6th & Park. Legend says witches cursed the city when their maple was cut for an 1889 May Day maypole, and the 1890 tornado followed. Locals still leave it offerings.

  • Pope Lick Trestle (Fisherville) · free · Google ↗

    Home of the Pope Lick Monster, Louisville's half-man, half-goat. Look from the ground ONLY. It's an active rail line and people have died on that trestle.

  • Hunter S. Thompson's Boyhood Home (Cherokee Triangle) · free · Google ↗

    2437 Ransdell Ave, where the inventor of gonzo journalism grew up. It's a private home, so wave from the sidewalk.

  • Caufield's Vampire Bat (Downtown) · free · Google ↗

    A 36-foot bat (the flying kind) clinging to Caufield's Novelty at 10th & Main. Built in 2000 as a prank on the baseball bat down the street.

  • Cave Hill's Famous Graves (Cave Hill) · free · Google ↗

    Colonel Sanders, Muhammad Ali, George Rogers Clark, Pappy Van Winkle and Patty and Mildred Hill, who wrote the 'Happy Birthday' tune. Grab a free map at the office.
