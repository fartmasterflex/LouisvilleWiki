Attractions & Outings

Things to do when you have guests in town, or just need to get out.

Icons & Green Spaces

  • The Belle of Louisville · Google ↗

    The oldest operating Mississippi-style steamboat in the world.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Louisville Zoo · Google ↗

    134 acres. The Glacier Run and Gorilla Forest are world-class.

  • Waterfront Botanical Gardens · Google ↗

    Built on top of a former landfill, now a stunning oasis overlooking the river.

    Also on: Parks

  • Cave Hill Cemetery · free · Google ↗

    An incredibly beautiful Victorian garden cemetery. Visit Ali and Colonel Sanders.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • The Big Four Bridge · free · Google ↗

    The pedestrian bridge connecting Louisville to Indiana. Best walked at sunset.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Churchill Downs · Google ↗

    Live racing in the spring and fall meets, not just Derby Day. Grandstand renovated for Derby 150.

    Also on: Sports, Clubs & Activities, Oldest Landmarks

  • Waterfront Park · free · Google ↗

    85 riverfront acres downtown. The West End expansion's first piece, PlayPort, opened in 2025.

    Also on: Parks

  • Whiskey Row · free · Google ↗

    The W Main St blocks of 1800s cast-iron and bourbon distilleries. Walk it end to end.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Louisville Slugger Field · Google ↗

    Home of the Bats. Cheap seats, river views, and Thirsty Thursdays.

Entertainment & Oddities

  • Louisville Mega Cavern · Google ↗

    A massive former limestone quarry. Ziplines, a ropes course, and holiday lights, all underground.

  • Idlewild Butterfly Farm · Google ↗
  • Vernon Lanes · Google ↗

    Oldest bowling alley in KY (bowling here since about 1918). Reopened in 2022: great food, historic lanes.

    Also on: Dining Directory, Oldest Landmarks

  • Recbar · Google ↗

    Jeffersontown. Massive arcade bar with 130+ vintage cabinets and pinball machines.

    Also on: Dining Directory

  • Kentucky Kingdom · Google ↗

    The amusement and water park at the fairgrounds. Ride the Flying Fox, the new suspended coaster.

  • Topgolf · Google ↗

    Oxmoor. Climate-controlled driving range bays with food and drinks.

  • GoodBounce Pickleball Yard · Google ↗

    Outdoor and covered pickleball with a bar and food.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • Iroquois Amphitheater · Google ↗

    Outdoor concerts in a hollow of Iroquois Park.

    Also on: Music, Stage & Screen
