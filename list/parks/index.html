Other Notable Parks

  • Jefferson Memorial Forest · free · Google ↗

    Largest urban municipal forest in the US. Real trails with real elevation. Bring water.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • Parklands of Floyds Fork · free · Google ↗

    4,000 acres of new parks in the East End, meticulously designed over 20 years.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • Tom Sawyer State Park · free · Google ↗

    Sports fields, a BMX track, a dog park, trails, and the Louisville Astronomical Society's urban observatory.

  • Waterfront Botanical Gardens · Google ↗

    Built on a former landfill overlooking the Ohio River. Stunning.

    Also on: Attractions & Outings

  • Waterfront Park · free · Google ↗

    The riverfront front lawn, growing west into Portland. PlayPort opened in 2025.

    Also on: Attractions & Outings

  • Beckley Creek Park · free · Google ↗

    The Parklands' first park: the Egg Lawn, the Strand, and creek trails.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • Louisville Champions Park · free · Google ↗

    Wide-open River Road fields with a disc golf course.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • McNeely Lake Park · free · Google ↗

    South End lake for fishing and paddling, with trails and a dog park.

    Also on: Weird & Wonderful

  • Blackacre State Nature Preserve · free · Google ↗

    Historic farm and nature preserve near Jeffersontown, with hiking trails.

Hidden Gems

  • Caperton Swamp · free · Google ↗

    River Road. A rare urban wetland with a one-mile loop through woods and ponds. Feels impossibly wild for the city.

  • Thurman-Hutchins Park · free · Google ↗

    River Road riverfront trails. Excellent birdwatching and quiet riverside hiking.

  • Beargrass Creek State Nature Preserve · free · Google ↗

    Woods and creek trails tucked behind Joe Creason Park, next to the Louisville Nature Center.

  • Louisville Nature Center · Google ↗
  • Big Rock · free · Google ↗

    Cherokee Park. A giant boulder in Beargrass Creek; the city's favorite creek-wading spot.

  • Hogan's Fountain · free · Google ↗

    Cherokee Park. Art deco fountain and pavilion at the top of the hill.

  • Fairmount Falls · free · Google ↗

    Jefferson Memorial Forest. A seasonal waterfall at the end of a short hike, best after rain.

  • Bernheim Arboretum & Research Forest · Google ↗

    30 min south. 16,000 acres of forest, art installations, and Thomas Dambo's giant wooden trolls (Mama Loumari, Little Nis & Little Elina).

    Also on: Day Trips

  • Falls of the Ohio State Park · Google ↗

    Just across the bridge in Clarksville. Ancient fossilized coral reef — the largest exposed Devonian reef in the world.

    Also on: Day Trips

  • Creasey Mahan Nature Preserve · free · Google ↗

    Goshen, just past the East End. 174 acres of mature mixed forest with excellent creek wading.

  • Otter Creek Outdoor Recreation Area · Google ↗

    Meade County, about 45 min. Mountain biking and bluffs over the Ohio.
