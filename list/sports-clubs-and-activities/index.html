Active & Sports Leagues

  • LXC Sports · Search ↗

    The massive mainstream option. Kickball, dodgeball, softball, and flag football.

  • Louisville Hardcourt Bike Polo · Search ↗

    Shelby Park. Pickup games with no set schedule; check their socials.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • Derby City Run Club · Search ↗

    Meets at local breweries for runs. Very social; people usually stay for a pint after.

  • Louisville Race Club · Search ↗

    Free, no-pressure social 5K on the first and third Saturdays of the month.

  • Iroquois Hill Runners · Search ↗

    Long-running club behind many local races.

  • Louisville Darts · Search ↗

    Competitive and casual dart leagues around town.

  • Louisville APA Poolplayers Association · Search ↗

    The local chapter for amateur pool leagues, playing out of various dive bars and halls.

  • Louisville Roller Derby · Search ↗

    Flat-track roller derby (formerly Derby City Roller Girls), 20 seasons strong. Intensity and camaraderie.

  • Louisville Rowing Club · Search ↗

    Learn-to-row classes and crews on the Ohio. Hosts the Derby City Head Race each October.

The DIY & Maker Scene

  • LVL1 Hackerspace · Search ↗

    A 24/7 community lab for geeks, engineers, and artists. If you want to build a flamethrower or a robot, go here.

  • Falls City Community Bikeworks · Search ↗

    A DIY shop where you learn to fix your own bike using their tools and knowledge.

    Also on: Active Louisville

  • Louisville Tool Library · Search ↗

    Borrow a power drill or a ladder like you borrow a book.

    Also on: Books, Libraries & Schools

  • Payne Street Pottery · Search ↗

    Classes and open studio time. Pottery is a very 'zen' way to meet consistent faces.

Games & Nerd Culture

  • Louisville Board Gamers · Search ↗

    A huge, welcoming group that meets at various libraries and game shops (Heroes, Through the Decades).

  • Zanzabar Pinball League · Search ↗

    Competitive flipping at one of the best arcades in the region.

Nature & Outdoors

  • Louisville Hiking Club · Search ↗

    A very active group that organizes group hikes in Jefferson Memorial Forest and the Gorge.

  • Louisville Grotto · Search ↗

    The local chapter of the National Speleological Society. For people who like crawling through caves.

  • Beckham Bird Club · Search ↗

    For birdwatchers. They do field trips to Cave Hill and the Falls of the Ohio.

  • Louisville Grows · Search ↗

    Community gardening and tree planting. Great for meeting people who care about the environment.

Movement & Arts

  • Derby City Swing · Search ↗

    West Coast Swing dance weekend every January, with workshops and social dancing.

  • Louisville Country Dancers · Search ↗

    Contra Dancing at various church halls. Incredibly welcoming to beginners.

  • Louisville Improvisors · Search ↗

    The city's long-running improv troupe, with shows and classes.

    Also on: Music, Stage & Screen

  • Turners Circus · Search ↗

    Aerial silks, trapeze, and gymnastics. A very tight-knit 'circus family' vibe.

    Also on: Active Louisville

Combat & Competition

  • Louisville Fencing Center · Search ↗

    One of the strongest fencing programs in the region. Welcomes absolute beginners and competitive fencers alike.

  • Louisville TKO Boxing · Search ↗

    Youth boxing nonprofit on E Breckinridge St.

  • Columbia Gym · Search ↗

    Where 12-year-old Cassius Clay first learned to box. Now Spalding University's gym.

  • Louisville Chess Club · Search ↗

    Regular events, tournaments, and casual games. One of the more active chess communities in Kentucky.

The Strange & Obscure

  • Waverly Hills Sanatorium · Search ↗

    The massive, decaying epicenter of Louisville ghost lore. Overnight stays for the brave.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • American Printing House for the Blind · Search ↗

    Factory tours of a world-class braille publisher in Clifton, plus the new Dot Experience museum (Oct 2026).

    Also on: Museums & Galleries, Oldest Landmarks

Civic & Professional

  • YPAL (Young Professionals Association) · Search ↗

    The standard for networking if you are 20-40 and looking for social crossovers.

  • Neighborhood Associations · Search ↗
