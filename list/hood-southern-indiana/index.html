🍽️ Dining Directory

  • Aladdin's (International & Mediterranean · Southern Indiana · $$) · Google ↗

    Mediterranean & Middle Eastern plates — New Albany (37 Bank St). Family-owned since 2013.

  • Red Yeti (American (Upscale & Contemporary) · Southern Indiana · $$$) · Google ↗

    Appalachian-inspired: prime rib, shrimp & grits, fried chicken — Jeffersonville, a block from the Big Four Bridge. Chef Michael Bowe was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist.

  • The Exchange Pub + Kitchen (Pub & Bar · Southern Indiana · $$) · Google ↗

    Fish & chips, shrimp & grits — New Albany (118 W Main), in an 1875 livery stable.

🦇 Weird & Wonderful

  • Big Four Station Park (Jeffersonville, IN) · free · Google ↗

    The best skyline view of Louisville is from Indiana. Walk across the bridge and look back.

  • Louisville Astronomical Society Star Parties (Tom Sawyer State Park) · free · Site ↗ · Google ↗

    Free public star parties one Saturday a month, March to November, at the Urban Astronomy Center in Tom Sawyer Park or the dark-sky Baker Observatory in Curby, IN (about an hour). Weather decides, so check their Facebook first.
