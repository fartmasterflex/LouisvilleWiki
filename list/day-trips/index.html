Day Trips

Worthy escapes within 3 hours. Louisville is embarrassingly well-positioned for road trips.

Open the interactive version →

Under 1 Hour

  • Falls of the Ohio State Park · Search ↗

    Clarksville, IN — 10 min. Largest exposed Devonian-era fossil reef in the world. Walk on 390-million-year-old coral.

    Also on: Parks

  • Bernheim Arboretum & Research Forest · Search ↗

    Clermont, KY — 30 min. 16,000 acres of forest and Thomas Dambo's giant wooden trolls (Mama Loumari, Little Nis & Little Elina).

    Also on: Parks

  • Bardstown · Search ↗

    45 min. 'Bourbon Capital of the World.' Heaven Hill, Willett, Barton distilleries. The Old Talbott Tavern (est. 1779).

  • Jim Beam American Stillhouse · Search ↗

    Clermont, KY — 30 min. Beam's big campus sits right next to Bernheim Forest. Pair them for a perfect day.

  • Taylorsville Lake · Search ↗

    45 min. State park with a beautiful lake for kayaking, fishing, and swimming.

  • Corydon, IN · Search ↗

    45 min. Indiana's first state capital. The Battle of Corydon Civil War site. Good for a historical wander.

  • Huber's Orchard & Winery · Search ↗

    Starlight, IN — 30 min. U-pick fruit, a farm market, and a winery. Peak in fall.

  • Kentucky Highland Renaissance Festival · Search ↗

    Eminence, KY — 45 min. Late May through July weekends.

    Also on: Notable Annual Events

1–2 Hours

  • Mammoth Cave National Park · Search ↗

    1.5 hrs south. The world's longest known cave system. The Historic Tour (Rotunda, Bottomless Pit, Fat Man's Misery) is unmissable.

  • Maker's Mark Distillery · Search ↗

    Loretto, KY — 1.25 hrs. Dip your own bottle in the red wax. Gorgeous campus.

  • Four Roses Distillery · Search ↗

    Lawrenceburg, KY — 1 hr. Spanish Mission-style distillery on the Salt River.

  • Wild Turkey Distillery · Search ↗

    Lawrenceburg, KY — 1 hr. Visitor center perched over the Kentucky River.

  • Abbey of Gethsemani · Search ↗

    Trappist, KY — 1 hr. Thomas Merton's monastery. Quiet grounds and monk-made fudge.

  • Abraham Lincoln Birthplace · Search ↗

    Hodgenville, KY — 1 hr. Memorial building over a symbolic cabin, plus the Sinking Spring.

  • Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill · Search ↗

    Harrodsburg, KY — 1.25 hrs. Restored Shaker village, trails, and an inn.

  • Keeneland · Search ↗

    Lexington — 1.25 hrs. April and October race meets; free morning workouts. Pair it with the Kentucky Horse Park.

  • Madison, IN & Clifty Falls · Search ↗

    1 hr. A preserved 1800s river town, plus waterfalls and gorge trails at Clifty Falls State Park.

  • Marengo Cave · Search ↗

    Marengo, IN — 45 min. Walking tours of a showcase cave. Squire Boone Caverns is nearby too.

  • Red River Gorge & Natural Bridge · Search ↗

    2 hrs southeast. The best hiking in the region: sandstone arches, rock shelters, excellent climbing, and the 78-foot Natural Bridge.

  • Lexington, KY · Search ↗

    1.25 hrs east. Horse country, Keeneland Racecourse, and Woodford Reserve just down the road.

  • Buffalo Trace & Woodford Reserve · Search ↗

    1–1.5 hrs east. Two of America's most iconic distilleries. Woodford's stone buildings and copper pot stills are stunning.

  • Cincinnati, OH · Search ↗

    1.5 hrs northeast. World-class art museum (free), Over-the-Rhine neighborhood, Skyline chili (mandatory).

  • French Lick & West Baden Springs · Search ↗

    1.25 hrs. The domed 1902 West Baden Springs Hotel is jaw-dropping. Casino and golf next door.

  • Holiday World & Splashin' Safari · Search ↗

    Santa Claus, IN — 1.25 hrs. Wooden coasters and free soft drinks.

  • National Corvette Museum · Search ↗

    Bowling Green, KY — 1.75 hrs. Includes the sinkhole that swallowed eight Corvettes in 2014.

2–3 Hours

  • Nashville, TN · Search ↗

    3 hrs south. If you've never been. Honky tonks, fantastic food scene, Ryman Auditorium.

  • Land Between the Lakes · Search ↗

    3 hrs southwest. 170,000 acres of national recreation area on twin lakes. Fantastic for stargazing.
