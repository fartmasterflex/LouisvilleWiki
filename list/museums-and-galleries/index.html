The Heavy Hitters

  • Speed Art Museum · Search ↗

    The oldest and largest art museum in Kentucky. Pay-what-you-can Sundays; check which ones, since the schedule changed in 2026.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Frazier History Museum · Search ↗

    The official starting point of the Bourbon Trail.

  • Muhammad Ali Center · Search ↗

    A spectacular, multi-story interactive museum dedicated to the Greatest.

  • Kentucky Derby Museum · Search ↗

    Everything Churchill Downs, right next to the track.

  • Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory · Search ↗

    Look for the 120-foot baseball bat leaning against the building.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Roots 101 African American Museum · Search ↗

    Dedicated to telling the story of the African American journey from Africa to Kentucky.

  • KMAC Museum · Search ↗

    Contemporary art focusing on craft and material.

  • Kentucky Science Center · Search ↗

    Great for kids, but the 4-story theater is great for everyone.

  • Louisville Water Tower · Search ↗

    WaterWorks Museum inside the 1860 pumping station, the world's oldest ornamental water tower.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Conrad-Caldwell House · Search ↗

    Old Louisville's Richardsonian Romanesque 'castle' on St. James Court. Guided tours.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Filson Historical Society · Search ↗

    Kentucky's oldest historical society (1884). Exhibits, lectures, and the Ferguson Mansion.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Locust Grove · Search ↗

    18th-century farm and final home of George Rogers Clark. Great living-history days.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Farmington · Search ↗

    1816 hemp plantation house built from a Thomas Jefferson design, with honest tours of the enslaved community.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Riverside, the Farnsley-Moremen Landing · Search ↗

    1837 farmhouse on a working river landing in the southwest. Free grounds.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • SAR Education Center & Museum · Search ↗

    Museum Row (809 W Main). American Revolution museum; partial opening July 2026, full opening 2027.

  • AHOY Children's Museum · Search ↗

    Portland. Children's museum on the Portland Museum campus; first phase due late 2026.

Independent Galleries & Niche Museums

  • 21c Museum Hotel · Search ↗

    A free, 24/7 contemporary art museum disguised as a boutique hotel.

  • American Printing House for the Blind · Search ↗

    Braille-book factory tours, plus the new Dot Experience museum opening Oct 30, 2026.

    Also on: Sports, Clubs & Activities, Oldest Landmarks

  • Portland Museum · Search ↗

    A deep dive into the river heritage and history of the Portland neighborhood.

  • Thomas Edison House · Search ↗

    He lived in Louisville briefly; this shotgun house explores his time here.

    Also on: Oldest Landmarks

  • Mellwood Art Center · Search ↗

    A massive former slaughterhouse turned into a maze of artist studios.

  • Louisville Stoneware · Search ↗

    Paristown. Watch the pottery get made and paint your own piece.

    Also on: Shops & Digging

  • Revelry Boutique Gallery · Search ↗

    NuLu spot championing hyper-local artists.

  • Garner Narrative · Search ↗

    Intimate gallery in NuLu with a focus on contemporary, provocative pieces.

  • Howard Steamboat Museum · Search ↗

    Gorgeous Victorian mansion detailing riverboat history (Jeffersonville).
