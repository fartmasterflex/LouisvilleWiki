Parks & Trails

  • The Louisville Loop · free · Google ↗

    A paved trail system planned to wrap ~100 miles around the city. About half is built.

  • The Parklands of Floyds Fork · free · Google ↗

    4,000 acres of meticulously designed new parks in the East End.

    Also on: Parks

  • Jefferson Memorial Forest · free · Google ↗

    The largest municipal urban forest in the US. Real, elevation-heavy hiking.

    Also on: Parks

  • Cherokee Park Loop · free · Google ↗

    The 2.4-mile scenic loop designed by Frederick Law Olmsted.

  • Beckley Creek Park · free · Google ↗

    The first park of the Parklands, with the Egg Lawn, trails, and the Strand.

    Also on: Parks

  • Louisville Champions Park · free · Google ↗

    River Road. Open fields, a disc golf course, and the Louisville Loop running by.

    Also on: Parks

Adrenaline & Movement

  • David Armstrong Extreme Park · free · Google ↗

    40,000 sq ft concrete skate park, open 6 AM to 11 PM daily. Famous for its massive 24-foot full pipe.

  • Climb NuLu · Google ↗

    Bouldering gym right in the middle of the NuLu neighborhood.

  • Rocksport · Google ↗

    Jeffersontown. The city's only rope-climbing gym (top-rope and lead). Reopened in 2026 under new climber-owners.

  • Louisville Hardcourt Bike Polo · Google ↗

    Polo on fixed-gear bikes in Shelby Park. No set schedule, so ask around or check their socials.

    Also on: Sports, Clubs & Activities

  • Turners Circus · Google ↗

    Learn aerial silks, trapeze, and gymnastics at the historic Turners club.

    Also on: Sports, Clubs & Activities

  • GoodBounce Pickleball Yard · Google ↗

    Open-play and league pickleball with a bar attached.

    Also on: Attractions & Outings

Waterways

  • Paddling Floyds Fork · Google ↗

    Rent a kayak from Blue Moon Canoe & Kayak and float down the scenic creek.

  • Harrods Creek · Google ↗

    A slow, beautiful creek feeding into the Ohio River. Rent a kayak from Nachand.
