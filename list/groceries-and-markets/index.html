Farmers Markets: Saturday Staples

  • Bardstown Road Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Highlands (1722 Bardstown Rd). Saturdays year-round in the church lots. The city's best-loved market.

  • Douglass Loop Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Belknap (2005 Douglass Blvd). Saturdays 10 to 2, spring through mid-December.

  • St. Matthews Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Beargrass Christian Church (4100 Shelbyville Rd). Saturday mornings, May through September.

  • Jeffersontown Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    J-Town Pavilion on Watterson Trail. Saturday mornings through October.

  • Old Louisville Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Central Park (6th & Park). Every other Saturday morning, May through November.

  • South End Community & Artisan Market · free · Search ↗

    Iroquois Park. Saturday mornings, mid-May through October.

  • East End Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Factory Lane. Tuesdays and Saturdays, plus a winter Saturday market.

Farmers Markets: Weeknights & Sundays

  • Phoenix Hill/NuLu Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    1007 E. Jefferson St. Tuesday afternoons, May through October.

  • Crescent Hill Community Market · free · Search ↗

    Crescent Hill United Methodist (201 S. Peterson Ave). Thursdays 3 to 6, June through October.

  • Gray Street Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Downtown medical campus. Thursday lunchtime, June through September.

  • West End Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    California Park (1600 St. Catherine St). Every other Sunday afternoon, bringing fresh food to the West End. Support this one.

  • Riverside Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Riverside, the Farnsley-Moremen Landing. Sunday mornings in summer.

  • Beulah Church Farmers Market · free · Search ↗

    Fern Creek. Year-round: Mondays in season, indoor Saturdays in winter.

  • Logan Street Market · free · Search ↗

    Shelby Park. Not a farmers market: a year-round indoor market hall with local makers and food stalls.
