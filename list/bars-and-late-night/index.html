4 AM Bars (Some or All Nights)

  • Mag Bar · Search ↗

    Old Louisville. Cheap beer, punk rock, malört, and a 4 AM last call every night.

    Open late

    Also on: Music, Stage & Screen

  • Nachbar · Search ↗

    Germantown. Dark, friendly, great jukebox. 3 PM to 4 AM daily.

    Open late

  • The Pearl · Search ↗

    Germantown. Drink wheel, jukebox, weekend DJs. 4 PM to 4 AM daily.

    Open late

  • Third Street Dive · Search ↗

    Downtown. Punk-rock dive with shows most nights.

    Open late

    Also on: Music, Stage & Screen

  • Hideaway Saloon · Search ↗

    Highlands. Video games, cheap drinks, upstairs view.

    Open late

    Open late

    Open late
