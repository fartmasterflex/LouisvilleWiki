Breweries & Distilleries

Local suds and world-class bourbon. Kentucky has more bourbon barrels aging than people living in it.

Craft Distilleries

  • Rabbit Hole Distillery · Google ↗

    NuLu. Stunning architecture, open-concept rickhouse. The Cavehill bourbon is exceptional.

  • Evan Williams Bourbon Experience · Google ↗

    Whiskey Row. Named after Louisville's first commercial distiller (1783). Great guided tour.

  • Old Forester Distillery · Google ↗

    Whiskey Row. America's first bottled bourbon (1870). Beautiful multi-floor tour experience.

  • Angel's Envy Distillery · Google ↗

    East Main, by Slugger Field. Finished in port wine casks. One of the most popular distilleries in the city.

  • Michter's Fort Nelson Distillery · Google ↗

    Whiskey Row. In the restored Fort Nelson Building. The bar upstairs is excellent.

  • Copper & Kings American Brandy · Google ↗

    Butchertown. Louisville's brandy distillery, back in local hands since 2025. Aged to music in sonic-maturation cellars; great rooftop bar.

  • Stitzel-Weller Distillery · Google ↗

    Shively. Home of Blade and Bow and the Bulleit Frontier Whiskey Experience. Gorgeous historic 1935 campus worth the drive.

  • Kentucky Peerless Distilling · Google ↗

    Downtown (120 N 10th St). A pre-Prohibition family name revived in 2015. Excellent rye.

  • Brough Brothers Distillery · Google ↗

    Kentucky's first Black-owned distillery, founded by three brothers.

  • Prova Spirits · Google ↗

    Phoenix Hill nano-distillery (opened 2025).

  • Chicken Cock Tasting Room · Google ↗

    NuLu (821 E Market). Tasting room and speakeasy for the revived 1856 whiskey brand.
